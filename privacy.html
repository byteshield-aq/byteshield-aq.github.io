Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What this app is

"ByteShield Uploader" is an internal tool used only by the ByteShield team to upload its own original videos to its own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

  • The only Google permission requested is youtube.upload, used to upload our own videos to our own channels.
  • We store only the OAuth token for our own account, on our own computer, to be able to upload.
  • The only API data we keep is the ID of the videos we upload, to link our Shorts to the full video.

Data we do not collect

We do not access, collect, store, sell or share any data of other YouTube users or viewers. We do not use cookies or analytics on this site.

Google API Services

ByteShield Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
